**Combined Summary of F1 and Motorsport News Research Findings**

**Formula 1 Championship and Driver Standings**
Kimi Antonelli is dominating the 2026 F1 season, leading the Drivers' Championship by a 43-point margin over George Russell. Antonelli has won the Spanish GP and the British GP sprint race, and took pole for the British GP, while both Russell and Lewis Hamilton have conceded their 2026 title chances. Max Verstappen is in strong form, and Lando Norris and McLaren are performing well, though Norris's Spanish GP victory was impacted by late VSC timing. 

**F1 Calendar, Regulations, and Team Updates**
The Azerbaijan GP opens a Formula 1 triple-header (Bahrain/Malaysia, then Singapore), with the 2026 championship potentially ending in Europe if Middle East conflicts prevent races in Qatar and Abu Dhabi. For the 2027 season, F1 will introduce reduced race distances and a Monaco Sprint race. Two-time champion Fernando Alonso has expressed frustration with the 2026 technical regulations' emphasis on battery management over high-speed racing at circuits like Silverstone, Spa, and Suzuka, and has held talks with FIA and F1 leadership about 2027 improvements. 

In driver and team news, Isack Hadjar returns to the Red Bull cockpit for the Azerbaijan GP, Liam Lawson returns to Racing Bulls, Yuki Tsunoda is back to reserve duties, and Elliott (former Mercedes tech chief) joins Alpine as CTO. Carlos Sainz has signed a multi-year deal with Williams F1, while Honda is replacing its engine chief following a poor start to the '26 season. Financially, F1 posted a record £400 million turnover, though profits fell slightly. On the technical and circuit front, the Madring circuit (Spanish GP debut) has been criticized as "very poor," and Lewis Hamilton raised concerns about a "light dimming" issue at Ferrari. Additionally, Max Verstappen took on the "Max vs 100" go-kart challenge.

*   **MotoGP:** Honda's 850cc prototypes topped tests, proving faster than 1000cc bikes. Somkiat Chantra replaced Joan Mir at Honda HRC Castrol, and Brad Binder secured a win at the Red Bull Ring.
